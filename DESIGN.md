# Design

## Theme

Lunar Light Awakening should feel like stepping into a mineral shop near closing time: warm lamps, darkened windows, quiet shelves, moonlit stone, and a person nearby who can help without pressure.

## Color

Use OKLCH tokens with a restrained, mineral-warm palette:

- Ink: deep aubergine-black for night surfaces, never pure black.
- Parchment: warm tinted neutral for light sections, never pure white.
- Moon gold: sparing accent for calls to action, rules, and active states.
- Amethyst: atmospheric dark surface color, used as depth rather than decoration.
- Sage: quiet secondary tint for wellness and botanical content.

Gold should feel rare. Large surfaces should be ink, parchment, or muted mineral color.

## Typography

Use a softer display serif for brand moments and a readable humanist sans for interface and body text. Avoid the default AI pairing of Inter plus dramatic italic serif. Headings can be elegant, but body copy must stay practical and easy to scan.

## Layout

Use roomy full-width bands and asymmetric editorial groupings rather than repeated identical card grids. Cards are acceptable for actionable repeated items such as practitioners, products, and blog posts, but avoid glass panels and nested cards.

## Components

- Navigation: translucent only when useful for readability, with clear touch targets.
- Buttons: pill shapes are acceptable for primary actions, with strong focus states and 44px minimum touch targets.
- Product and practitioner cards: image-led, tactile, and practical.
- Section headings: concise, with restrained dividers or eyebrow labels.
- Dialogs: used for practitioner detail only, with clear close affordance and keyboard escape support.

## Imagery

Use the real shop, product, practitioner, and blog imagery already in the project. Imagery should be decisive and readable, not buried under colored overlays.

## Motion

Keep motion slow, calm, and purposeful. Avoid randomized decorative animation. Respect reduced motion and do not animate layout properties.
