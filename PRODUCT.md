# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local visitors in and around Schofield, Wisconsin who are curious about crystals, intuitive wellness, alternative healing, workshops, and practitioner services. Many are browsing before visiting in person, checking hours, looking for a service, or trying to understand whether the shop feels welcoming and credible.

## Product Purpose

Lunar Light Awakening is a public-facing brand site for a wellness and crystal shop. The site should make the shop feel easy to visit, emotionally safe, and full of real things to explore: stones, jewelry, herbs, local art, classes, and healing practitioners.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, luminous. The brand should feel calm and spiritually open without drifting into vague fantasy. It should carry the tactility of a shop filled with minerals, wood shelves, candlelight, and practical guidance from real people.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic purple mystical templates, heavy glassmorphism, neon astrology aesthetics, corporate wellness polish, fake luxury spa styling, and pages that hide practical visit information behind decorative effects.

## Design Principles

- Make the shop feel physically visitable: use real imagery, clear location details, and grounded copy.
- Let lunar cues be quiet and intentional: mineral light, curved forms, dark sky, and warm highlights rather than novelty effects.
- Prioritize trust before wonder: readable text, visible hours, practical service paths, and real practitioner information.
- Keep the experience generous but not ornate: visual warmth should support browsing, not slow it down.
- Treat local community as a feature: classes, practitioners, and local artisan products should feel connected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, clear keyboard focus states, readable body text, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alt text, and practical contact paths for users who prefer phone, email, maps, or Facebook.
